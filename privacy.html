This Privacy Policy describes how EasyFame collects, uses, and protects user information.

By using the Application, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

1. What data we collect

The Application collects the minimum necessary data for its operation.

1.1. Data you provide voluntarily

  • Username and Display name
  • Selected gender (if provided)

This data is stored locally and can be changed at any time.

1.2. Data collected automatically

  • Technical device info (OS version, device type)
  • Anonymous usage statistics

2. Camera and Microphone

The App requests access to Camera to simulate live broadcasts. We do not record or store your video/audio on our servers.

3. Data Storage

All settings are stored locally. We do not transfer or sell your data to third parties.

4. Payments

Processed through Apple In-App Purchases. We do not have access to your card details.
